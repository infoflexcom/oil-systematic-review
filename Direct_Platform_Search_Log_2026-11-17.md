## Important limitations and next steps

The broad query is not the planned full Boolean strategy; different source search semantics mean raw counts are not comparable. The results overlap across sources and contain irrelevant and out-of-period material. Counts must not be added to create a PRISMA flow. Run and record source-specific Boolean searches and date filters, export complete records, deduplicate, and independently screen and log full-text decisions. Obtain Scopus, Web of Science and ScienceDirect exports through authorized institutional access. Do not send passwords or credentials. Full-text extraction, independent risk-of-bias assessment, PRISMA reconciliation and checklist completion remain outstanding.
